The Useless Machine

A little box with one single switch. Flip the switch on and the machine gets to work: the lid opens, a hand rises out of the box, pushes the switch back to OFF and retreats. It does absolutely nothing useful, and that is the whole point.

How it works

Inside the box there is a small motor, a cam and a lever. The switch you flip also closes the circuit, so the motor starts, the cam pushes a lever that opens the lid, the arm swings out and knocks the switch back to the off position. With the switch off, the motor stops and a spring pulls the arm home. Nothing else happens, ever.

Where it comes from

The idea is usually credited to Claude Shannon, who built a version he called the "Ultimate Machine" in the 1950s, and it was later popularised by the maker community. It is a joke machine: a device whose only purpose is to switch itself off.
